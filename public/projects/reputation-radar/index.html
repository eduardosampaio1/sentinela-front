Methodology and provenance

Phase 1

Readings use cycle averages (repetitions per prompt), never single executions — AI engines vary between identical runs. Scores and source grades are computed by deterministic, auditable code; no generative LLM assigns any grade. The LLM appears only in the labeled interpretive summary.
